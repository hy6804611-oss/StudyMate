🎖️ CHAPTER 1: INTRODUCTION TO THE NIGERIAN DEFENCE ACADEMY (NDA)

The Nigerian Defence Academy (NDA) is Nigeria's premier military university responsible for training officer cadets for the Nigerian Armed Forces. It is located in Kaduna State, Nigeria, and is one of the most respected military institutions in Africa.

The Academy combines military training, academic education, leadership development, and physical fitness to produce highly disciplined officers who will serve in the Nigerian Army, Nigerian Navy, and Nigerian Air Force.

🏛 History of the NDA

The Nigerian Defence Academy was established in 1964 shortly after Nigeria gained independence. Before then, Nigerian military officers received their training in foreign military institutions, especially in the United Kingdom.

The establishment of the NDA enabled Nigeria to train its own military officers locally while maintaining international military standards.

🎯 Mission of the NDA

The mission of the Nigerian Defence Academy is to:

✔ Train military officers with sound academic knowledge.

✔ Produce disciplined leaders with integrity.

✔ Develop officers capable of defending Nigeria.

✔ Promote professionalism in the Armed Forces.

✔ Build future military commanders and national leaders.

🌍 Vision of the NDA

The vision of the Academy is to become one of the best military universities in the world by producing competent officers equipped with military knowledge, leadership skills, and academic excellence.

📋 CHAPTER 3: NDA ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS

Admission into the Nigerian Defence Academy (NDA) is highly competitive. Every applicant must satisfy the academic, medical, physical and character requirements before being considered for admission.

🇳🇬 Citizenship Requirement

Applicants must be citizens of Nigeria by birth. Only Nigerian citizens are eligible to apply for admission into the Regular Combatant Course of the Nigerian Defence Academy.

🎂 Age Requirement

Applicants must fall within the age range specified by the NDA for the admission year.

✔ Meet the approved minimum age.

✔ Must not exceed the approved maximum age.

✔ Candidates outside the approved age range are not eligible.

📚 Educational Qualifications

Applicants must possess the required O'Level qualifications from recognized examination bodies.

Acceptable examination bodies include:

✔ WAEC

✔ NECO

✔ NABTEB (where applicable)

Applicants are expected to obtain at least the minimum required credits in the relevant subjects.

📝 JAMB Requirement

Every applicant must register for the Unified Tertiary Matriculation Examination (UTME) conducted by JAMB.

Applicants must:

✔ Choose the Nigerian Defence Academy during UTME registration where applicable.

✔ Obtain the minimum JAMB score required for the admission year.

✔ Participate in the NDA Screening Test after meeting the JAMB requirement.

📏 Physical Requirements

Military officers must be physically fit. Therefore, every candidate must satisfy the physical standards laid down by the Academy.

Applicants should possess:

✔ Good physical fitness.

✔ Healthy body condition.

✔ Good eyesight.

✔ Ability to participate in strenuous military training.

🩺 Medical Fitness

Medical examination checks include:

✔ General body health.

✔ Hearing test.

✔ Blood tests.

✔ Chest examination.

✔ Other medical assessments required by the Armed Forces.

👤 Character Requirement

Candidates are expected to demonstrate:

✔ Honesty.

✔ Discipline.

✔ Responsibility.

✔ Respect for authority.

📄 Required Documents

📝 CHAPTER 4: NDA SCREENING TEST AND SELECTION PROCESS

The admission process into the Nigerian Defence Academy (NDA) consists of several stages. Successfully meeting the admission requirements does not automatically guarantee admission. Every applicant must pass through a competitive selection process.

🖥 Stage 1: JAMB Registration

Every applicant must first register for the Unified Tertiary Matriculation Examination (UTME) conducted by the Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board (JAMB).

Applicants should:

✔ Register for UTME.

✔ Select the appropriate course.

✔ Meet the required UTME score.

✔ Keep all registration documents safely.

📄 Stage 2: NDA Application

After meeting the required JAMB score, eligible candidates should complete the NDA online application.

Applicants should:

✔ Complete the online application form carefully.

✔ Upload the required documents.

✔ Print the acknowledgement slip.

✔ Monitor official announcements.

💻 Stage 3: NDA Screening Test (CBT)

Qualified candidates are invited to write the NDA Screening Test. The examination is Computer-Based (CBT) and tests candidates on subjects related to their chosen course.

The screening test usually consists of:

✔ English Language.

✔ Mathematics (for Science courses).

✔ Physics.

✔ Geography.

✔ Government.

✔ History.

✔ Economics.

✔ Biology.

✔ Chemistry.

✔ Literature in English.

Note: The subjects depend on the candidate's chosen course.

📊 Stage 4: Release of Screening Results

After the screening examination, successful candidates are shortlisted based on their overall performance.

Only shortlisted candidates proceed to the next stage.

🏕 Stage 5: Armed Forces Selection Board (AFSB)

Shortlisted candidates are invited to attend the Armed Forces Selection Board (AFSB), which is one of the most important stages of the admission process.

During the AFSB, candidates undergo:

✔ Medical examination.

✔ Physical fitness assessment.

✔ Leadership assessment.

✔ Aptitude tests.

✔ Interview.

✔ Character evaluation.

✔ Documentation verification.

🏃 Physical Fitness Assessment

Candidates may be assessed in:

✔ Long-distance running.

✔ Push-ups.

✔ Sit-ups.

✔ Endurance exercises.

✔ General body fitness.

🩺 Medical Examination

Candidates must be medically fit to undergo military training.

Medical checks include:

✔ Vision.

✔ Hearing.

✔ Blood pressure.

✔ General health.

✔ Physical condition.

🎖 Final Selection

Candidates who successfully pass all stages are offered admission into the Nigerian Defence Academy and begin training as Officer Cadets.

💡 Preparation Tips

✔ Study consistently for both JAMB and the NDA Screening Test.

✔ Exercise regularly to improve physical fitness.

✔ Eat healthy meals.

✔ Sleep adequately before examinations.

✔ Arrive early at examination and interview venues.

✔ Remain disciplined and confident throughout the selection process.

⭐ Key Point

Success in the NDA selection process depends on a combination of academic excellence, physical fitness, medical fitness, discipline, leadership qualities, and good character.

❓ CHAPTER 9: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs) ABOUT THE NIGERIAN DEFENCE ACADEMY (NDA)

Many candidates have questions about the Nigerian Defence Academy (NDA). This chapter provides answers to some of the most common questions asked by applicants.

❓ 1. What is the Nigerian Defence Academy (NDA)?

The Nigerian Defence Academy is Nigeria's premier military university responsible for training officer cadets for the Nigerian Army, Nigerian Navy, and Nigerian Air Force.

❓ 2. Is the NDA a university?

Yes. The NDA is a degree-awarding university. Cadets graduate with a Bachelor's Degree and are commissioned as military officers after successfully completing their training.

❓ 3. Can both males and females apply?

Yes. Qualified male and female Nigerian citizens who meet the admission requirements are eligible to apply.

❓ 4. Is admission into the NDA free?

Cadets admitted into the Regular Combatant Course receive government-sponsored military and academic training in accordance with the Academy's policies.

❓ 5. Can I apply without writing JAMB?

No. Applicants are required to participate in the Unified Tertiary Matriculation Examination (UTME) and satisfy the applicable admission requirements.

❓ 6. Can I combine two O'Level results?

Applicants should always follow the admission requirements published by the NDA for the particular admission year regarding acceptable O'Level results and combinations.

❓ 7. What happens after passing the NDA Screening Test?

Successful candidates are shortlisted and invited to attend the Armed Forces Selection Board (AFSB), where further medical, physical, and interview assessments are conducted.

❓ 8. Will I become a military officer immediately after admission?

No. Candidates first undergo military and academic training. Only those who successfully complete the programme are commissioned as officers.

❓ 9. Can I choose the military service I want?

Cadets are eventually commissioned into one of the three Armed Services based on the needs of the Armed Forces and applicable policies.

❓ 10. How important is physical fitness?

Physical fitness is extremely important. Military training is demanding, and applicants should begin exercising regularly long before the screening and selection process.

❓ 11. Can I apply again if I am not admitted?

Yes. Candidates who remain eligible may apply again during a future admission exercise after improving their preparation and meeting the requirements for that year.

❓ 12. What qualities should every NDA applicant possess?

✔ Leadership.

✔ Patriotism.
