🔐 StudyMate Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

1. Introduction

Welcome to StudyMate.

StudyMate is an educational application designed to help students learn, revise, practise and organize their studies.

This Privacy Policy explains what information StudyMate may process, why that information is needed, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

By using StudyMate, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

3. Information StudyMate May Process

Account information

When you create or use a StudyMate account, StudyMate may process information associated with your account, such as your email address and Firebase authentication identifier.

This information is used to authenticate your account and keep different users' accounts separate.

Profile information

StudyMate allows users to provide profile information such as:

  • Display name
  • Country
  • Profile picture

Study planning information

StudyMate may store information that a student enters into the Study Planner or Advanced Study Planner, including study tasks, subjects, dates, times, durations, selected subjects, exam dates, reminder times and completion status.

Learning activity

StudyMate may maintain information needed for learning features such as study progress, achievements, levels, badges and study streaks.

4. Profile Pictures

If you choose to add a profile picture, StudyMate uses that picture to display your profile.

In the current web version, profile information such as the profile picture may be stored locally on the device using browser storage.

Users should only upload pictures they have the right to use.

5. Local Device Storage

Some StudyMate features use local device or browser storage to remember information between sessions.

This can include information such as profile preferences, study plans, planner settings, reminder records, achievements and other application settings.

Local storage is used to make StudyMate features continue working when the user returns to the application.

6. Firebase Authentication

StudyMate uses Firebase Authentication to provide account sign-in and account management.

Firebase may process account information such as an email address and authentication identifiers required to provide the authentication service.

StudyMate does not use another user's account information simply because multiple accounts are used on the same device. Each authenticated user has a separate Firebase account identity.

7. Notifications

StudyMate may request permission to send notifications so that it can remind students about scheduled study activities.

Notifications may be used for features such as study reminders and planner reminders.

Notification permission is controlled by the user's device or browser settings.

Users can disable notifications through the applicable device or application settings.

8. How StudyMate Uses Information

Information processed by StudyMate may be used to:

  • Create and authenticate user accounts.
  • Display the user's StudyMate profile.
  • Save study plans and planner settings.
  • Provide study reminders.
  • Track learning progress and achievements.
  • Provide learning, revision, quiz, flashcard and CBT features.
  • Maintain the functionality and security of StudyMate.
  • Respond to privacy or support requests.

9. Sharing of Information

StudyMate does not sell users' personal information.

StudyMate may use service providers or technology providers when necessary to operate features of the application.

For example, Firebase may be used for authentication and related account services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, security and safety of StudyMate, its users or others.

10. Data Security

StudyMate takes reasonable measures to protect information used by the application.

Account authentication is provided through Firebase Authentication.

Users should also protect their account credentials and should not share passwords or authentication information with other people.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Data Retention

StudyMate retains information for as long as it is reasonably necessary to provide the relevant features, maintain an account, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the security of the application.

Information stored locally on a user's device may remain there until the user clears the relevant application or browser storage, removes the information through available StudyMate controls, or uninstalls the application where applicable.

13. Children and Younger Users

StudyMate is an educational service intended for students.

We aim to handle student information responsibly and to comply with applicable privacy and child protection laws.

Where applicable law requires parental or guardian consent for the collection or processing of a child's personal information, that requirement must be followed.

StudyMate does not intentionally request precise location, contacts, microphone recordings, or similar information merely to provide ordinary study features.

Google Play requires additional privacy and data practices when children are included in an app's target audience, so StudyMate's Play Console declarations and actual data practices must remain consistent with this policy.

14. Third-Party Services

StudyMate may rely on third-party services that help provide application functionality.

These services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

StudyMate will only use such services for purposes consistent with the functionality of the application and applicable requirements.

15. Advertising

If advertising or an advertising SDK is introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the applicable Google Play Data Safety disclosures will be updated to accurately explain the relevant data practices before or when the feature is introduced.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

StudyMate may update this Privacy Policy when the application, its services, or applicable privacy requirements change.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Users are encouraged to review this policy periodically.
